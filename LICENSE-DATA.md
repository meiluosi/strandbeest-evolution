# 实测与实验数据许可说明 · Measurement & Experimental Data Licensing (CC0 1.0)

本仓库中的实验数据与实测数据集（如 `experiments/`、`data/measurements/` 以及默认按项目许可提交的社区 `Measurement` 数据）采用 **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication** 贡献至公有领域。

任何人均可在不寻求许可、不承担法律署名义务的前提下，自由复制、修改、分发或将这些数据用于校准、训练、仿真对比及学术/商业用途。

**科学溯源与自愿署名（Scientific Provenance & Optional Credit）**：
虽然法律上采用 CC0 免除了强制署名义务以降低数据共享阻力，但本项目的证据分级体系（`docs/VALIDATION.md` 的 E3 判据）要求保留完整的**科学出处**（仪器、标定、日期、不确定度及可选的贡献者称呼）。我们在实验报告与校准档案中会如实记录并致谢数据来源。社区贡献者也可在提交时显式选择 `CC-BY-4.0`。

---

The experimental and physical measurement datasets in this repository (including `experiments/`, `data/measurements/`, and community `Measurement` submissions contributed under the default project license) are dedicated to the public domain under the **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication**.

You may copy, modify, distribute, and use the data for calibration, training, benchmarking, or any other purpose without asking permission and without a legal attribution requirement.

**Scientific Provenance & Optional Credit**:
While CC0 removes legal attribution friction, our evidence-grading system (`docs/VALIDATION.md` E3 criteria) requires **scientific provenance** (instrument, calibration, date, uncertainty, and optional contributor credit). We record and credit data sources in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md` and calibration profiles. Contributors may also explicitly opt into `CC-BY-4.0` per submission.
